# Suggested story for an eight-page report

The defensible contribution is a controlled test of when composing separately
measured local kernels predicts interacting agents. Omitting state can lose a
particular outcome law, while a successful late-outcome forecast can still miss
earlier dynamics. Keep the designated Llama primary null prominent; the sharper
OLMo mechanism is a labelled exploratory case study.

## Short wording the team can adapt

“In the designated Llama n=10 full-history cheap-talk comparison, retaining signals
did not demonstrably improve final-five-round CRPS. The original difference was
−0.000086, with a conditional 95% interval spanning zero. Independent simulation and
table-resampling sensitivities preserve that endpoint conclusion. Both finite-state
audits nevertheless miss early dynamics, so endpoint performance does not establish
trajectory calibration.”

“An exploratory OLMo n=4 last-round-only condition separates the composed laws.
Enumerated signal-aware and actions-only chains assign probabilities 0.14239 and
0.00007636 to every agent bidding high throughout rounds 16–20; 8 of 50 live runs
show that event. The observed paired CRPS improvement is 0.0252, with a 95% run-level
interval [−0.0034, 0.0539]. The exact probability contrast and uncertain live score
contrast answer different questions.”

“In exact cached-kernel ablations, retaining own-message state nearly recovers the
full late-outcome law, whereas retaining neighbour messages alone does not.
Replacing observations with the literal none token preserves a near-full law.
The result depends on which information is retained and how omitted messages are
averaged; it does not show neighbour messages are necessary for coordination or
that agents form commitments.”

“The finite-horizon TV theorem applies to true kernels under independent conditional
sampling and compatible coarse wiring. Its logged RHS is an estimate and saturates
early here. Exact transfer needs zero population defect and full-history coverage.
Neither a finite zero estimate nor a small endpoint score supplies a safety certificate.”

## At most three main figures

1. **Primary result and missing dynamics.** Llama n=10 empirical X_t and both audit
   trajectories, a panel of binned TV with simultaneous null thresholds, and a small
   endpoint-D interval. Label conditional uncertainty and show MC sensitivity
   separately. Annotate bound saturation; avoid a large B_t axis hiding the gap.
2. **Exploratory state retention.** OLMo n=4 last-1 full final-five-round distribution
   for full/action-only laws and observed counts; P(F=1) for both/own-only/neighbours-
   only/neither; a small own-message occupancy versus synthetic weighting panel.
   Separate exact-model probabilities from live binomial intervals. Label selection
   after earlier results.
3. **Averaging versus none.** Compare the averaged laws with deterministic own-none,
   neighbour-none and both-none laws on one P(F=1) scale. Mark cached-law results as
   model-based counterfactuals. Replace this figure with size-by-memory only if
   completed live cells add a stronger result with appropriate independent-run
   intervals; CPU forecasts must not be presented as live cells.

An eight-page allocation could give one page to question/related work, two to setup
and protocol, two to the primary results, two to the exploratory case study, and one
to limitations. Move full kernel grids, proof, gate chronology and sensitivities
to the appendix/supplement. This is an outline, not a rewritten paper.

## Verified related-work comparison

**Flint, Aiello, Pastor-Satorras and Baronchelli (2026):** PNAS 123(34), e2531697123,
DOI 10.1073/pnas.2531697123. Its [primary full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13505991/)
describes finite memory H=5, restricted-token policies cached for all memory states,
and empirical validation against direct LLM generations. It studies naming-game
bias and population-size effects. A fair distinction is this project's transfer
from synthetic audits to full-history swarm histories and its explicit state
omission. Do not imply the earlier study lacked live validation, or claim the
first composition of cached LLM policies or study of collective-size effects.

**METR (26 August 2026):** the [primary incident report](https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/)
describes unsanctioned communication and coordinated cheating/attack activity in a
benchmark context. It motivates studying interactions; this repeated game does not
reproduce that incident or validate its mechanisms. Narrow “completely unprompted”
to the supported description and preserve the report's limitations.

**Perturbation theory:** the setup's commented [Mitrophanov (2005)](https://doi.org/10.1239/jap/1134587812)
reference is real: J. Appl. Prob. 42(4), 1003–1014, on uniformly ergodic-chain
sensitivity and convergence. It does not establish uniform trajectory guarantees
for this full-history system. The review's finite-horizon telescoping proof stands
on its own; frame it as a specialised use of standard probability arguments rather
than a verified general novelty claim.

## Limitations to retain

One prompt/payoff family, constrained labels, limited graph evidence, two models
with the second selected after results, 50 independent runs per principal condition,
shared fitted audit tables, synthetic averaging, and exploratory multiplicity.
Distinguish binary-action from six-outcome joint TV. The OLMo full-history all-low
endpoint is uninformative about incremental signal value; collapsed intervals do
not mean high precision. Keep the original failed gate, exception and confirmation.
Every exact result is conditional on its specified kernel and state space.
