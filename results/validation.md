**How each expected result was judged** (my reading of the hand-off; Abdul may prefer another):

- "ε = 0": pooled ε(p_test) and the mean swarm defect ε(p_swarm) below 1e-12.
- "Δ inside the noise floor": the largest Δ_t over the 20 rounds is below the 95th
  percentile of that same maximum between two model samples of the swarm's size (a
  simultaneous floor; with a per-round floor, about 1 round in 20 exceeds it by chance).
- "Bound above the measured Δ" (Two-round memory): B_t ≥ Δ_t from round 2 on, and
  Δ_t ≤ B_t + the per-round floor in every round. In round 1 the bound is 0 because START is
  exact, while the measured Δ_1 is sampling noise alone.
- At n = 4, the swarm's law of y is also tested against the exact law of the chain the
  policy should match (G5's per-run test, 99th percentile of 20,000 simulated sets), and a
  last-round-only run goes through the full G5 check against an enumerated table.

**Finding:** for Two-round memory at n = 4, the per-run test rejects the actions-only
chain (gap 0.124 against a threshold of 0.101), as it should, since the chain forgets the
second round. It is not part of that policy's pass criterion.

**Scripted-policy details the hand-off left open:** Copy is written as P(H) = 0.2 + 0.6 f
(0.8, 0.5 and 0.2 on the cycle); Hidden signal bids H with probability 0.5 in round 1, and
the mass not on "high" is split evenly between "low" and silence (0.15/0.15 after H,
0.45/0.45 after L). The run logs (34 MB) are in `data/runs/harness/`, which is git-ignored;
they are reproduced exactly from the seeds by rerunning the script.
