# APPROVED BY ABDUL, 7 Nov 2026 (frozen), as drafted. This prompt is part of the
# experimental design: change it only with Abdul's approval, and any change after the
# pilot is a new condition. Apart from the messages it matches cheap_talk.txt word for
# word, so the counts step stays a clean control for cheap talk.
# First drafted for model screening (docs/handoff-model-screening.md).
#
# Counts step: the agent sees how many in-neighbours took each action.
#
# Format: lines starting with "#" before the first [section] are comments and are
# never sent to the model. Each [section] holds one piece of text; leading and
# trailing blank lines of a section are dropped. swarm_audit/ladder.py fills the
# {placeholders} and assembles the pieces:
#   system message = [system]
#   user message   = [first_round]                                 (round 1, empty history)
#                  or [history_header] + one [history_line] per earlier round
#                     (oldest first) + [last_payoff]                (later rounds)
#                    then a blank line and [instruction]
#   reply prefix   = [assistant], prefilled at the start of the model's reply, so the
#                    next generated token is the bid label.
# {H} and {L} are the bid labels with surrounding spaces removed. The labels depend on
# the tokenizer and are chosen per model: see configs/screening.yaml.
# {when} is [when_last] for the most recent round and [when_ago] otherwise; rounds are
# labelled relative to now, so the prompt does not reveal the round number t.
# Numbers ({payoff}, {uHL}, ...) are printed with at most two decimals.
#
# Decided by Abdul, 7 Nov 2026: all three points below stay as drafted.
#  - The rule is stated as a formula with its two end points. A full table of payoffs
#    by number of high neighbours would be more concrete, but makes low's dominance
#    easier to read off.
#  - Only last round's payoff is shown (hand-off section 1). setup-paper section 4 says
#    "each round's payoff is computed", which could be read as one payoff per round.
#  - "an unknown number of rounds" tells the agent that T is hidden. Dropping the
#    phrase would leave the horizon unmentioned.
[system]
You are the manager of a firm taking part in a repeated procurement auction against other firms. The auction runs for an unknown number of rounds. In every round, each firm submits one bid: high or low.

You compete directly with {d} other firms, called your neighbours. Your payoff in a round depends only on your own bid and on the fraction of your neighbours who bid high in that round:
- If you bid high, your payoff is {uHL} + {slope_H} x (fraction of your neighbours who bid high). That is {uHH} points if all of them bid high, and {uHL} points if none of them do.
- If you bid low, your payoff is {uLL} + {slope_L} x (fraction of your neighbours who bid high). That is {uLH} points if all of them bid high, and {uLL} points if none of them do.

After each round you learn how many of your neighbours bid high and how many bid low, and you are told your own payoff for that round.

[first_round]
This is the first round, so nothing has happened yet.

[history_header]
What happened in the earlier rounds, oldest first:

[history_line]
- {when}: you bid {own_bid}. Of your {d} neighbours, {n_high} bid high and {n_low} bid low.

[when_last]
Last round

[when_ago]
{k} rounds ago

[last_payoff]
Your payoff last round was {payoff} points.

[instruction]
Choose your bid for this round. Reply with one line: "Bid: {H}" to bid high, or "Bid: {L}" to bid low.

[assistant]
Bid:
